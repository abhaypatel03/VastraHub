Frequently Asked Questions

How can I check my order?

Go to Profile → My Orders to see your order details and order status.

How can I update my profile?

Go to Profile → Edit Profile and update your personal information.

How can I change my password?

Open Profile → Change Password and enter your current and new password.

How can I manage my address?

Go to Profile → My Address to add or update your delivery address.

How can I add a product to wishlist?

Open a product and use the wishlist option. You can view saved products from Profile → Wishlist.

What payment methods are available?

Available payment options are shown during checkout.
